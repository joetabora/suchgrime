# SuchGrime Design System

## Philosophy

Stunning, rule-bending, unique. Traditional tattoo flash energy meets long-and-low chopper culture and Milwaukee garage grit. High contrast, dramatic type, editorial layouts with deliberate tension. Premium but filthy. Should impress both riders and designers.

## Color

- bg-base: #0A0A0A
- bg-surface: #141414
- bg-elevated: #1C1C1C
- text-primary: #F5F5F0
- text-secondary: #A3A3A3
- accent-blood: #C41E3A
- accent-chrome: #E8E8E8
- accent-gold: #D4AF37
- accent-flash: #FF2E2E
- border: #2A2A2A

Use blood red and gold sparingly for emphasis. Chrome for highlights and metallic feel. High contrast always.

## Typography

- Display / Headlines: Bebas Neue (tight tracking, large sizes, dramatic)
- Subheads / Accents: Oswald
- Body: Inter
- Mono / Details: JetBrains Mono
- Occasional decorative: Pirata One for flash-style elements only

Scale aggressively. Headlines can be huge. Mix weights and sizes for editorial tension.

## Spacing & Layout

- Generous negative space mixed with tight clusters.
- Asymmetric grids where it serves the content.
- Full-bleed sections for impact.
- Card-based for shop and events, but with strong typography overlays.



## Components

- Event cards: Date large, title, location, “I’m Rolling” CTA
- Product cards: Strong image, price, limited-drop badge when relevant
- Build features: Editorial layout, large imagery, story-first
- Buttons: High contrast, sharp or slightly rounded, blood or chrome variants
- Navigation: Minimal, bold type



## Voice

Direct, confident, no fluff. “Ride Ugly. Die Pretty.” energy. Garage real, not corporate.  
  
  
  
## Aesthetic Reference Direction

Hybrid of [actuallyitsaxel.com](http://actuallyitsaxel.com) (raw, personal, rider-owned brand energy) and [toxico.co.uk](http://toxico.co.uk) (bold graphic streetwear, high-impact merch visuals, tattoo/lowbrow edge).

- Product and merch should feel front-and-center, not secondary.

- Imagery treatment: high-contrast, slightly gritty, strong graphic overlays.

- Layout can be denser in shop and product areas; keep editorial breathing room on features and builds.

- Allow controlled chaos: overlapping elements, tight type, occasional flash-style decorative marks.

- Avoid generic luxury or corporate motorcycle aesthetics.
