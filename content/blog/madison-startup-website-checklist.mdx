Madison startups move fast — your website should too. Before you launch, run through this checklist to avoid rebuilds six months later.

## Technical foundation

- **Next.js or equivalent** SSR/SSG for SEO and performance
- Mobile-first layout with Core Web Vitals above 90
- Canonical URLs, Open Graph, and XML sitemap from day one
- Structured data: Organization, WebSite, and Service where relevant

## Conversion paths

- Clear primary CTA above the fold
- Contact or booking flow connected to email/CRM
- FAQ section targeting how buyers search in Madison

## Plan for growth

Startups outgrow brochure sites quickly. Build with a **CMS or structured content model** so you can add location pages, case studies, and blog posts without rewriting the codebase.
