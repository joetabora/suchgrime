Most small businesses lose hours every week on the same manual tasks — copying form submissions into spreadsheets, sending confirmation emails, updating booking statuses.

Business automation connects your existing tools and builds custom systems where gaps exist. Here's where to start:

## Audit your repetitive tasks

Track one week of operations. Note every task that follows the same steps: intake forms, appointment reminders, invoice follow-ups, status updates.

## Connect before you rebuild

Tools like Zapier and Make can wire together apps you already use. A new form submission can trigger a CRM entry, Slack notification, and calendar block — without custom code.

## Build when integrations fall short

When off-the-shelf automation isn't enough, custom dashboards and internal apps fill the gap. Booking admin panels, client tracking, and ops dashboards built with React and PostgreSQL give you full control.

## Measure the ROI

Track time saved per week after each automation ships. Most clients see payback within the first month on high-frequency workflows.
