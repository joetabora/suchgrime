The most common programmatic SEO mistake: **indexing pages that no human (or crawler) can reach through HTML links**.

## Sitemaps aren't enough

Google may discover URLs from XML sitemaps, but internal links distribute authority and confirm relevance. Matrix pages like `/locations/denver/business-automation` must be linked from parent hubs.

## Build visible hub grids

On every location page, list all service combinations. On service pillar pages, link to top markets. Footer and nav should point to `/services`, `/locations`, and `/industries` — not hash anchors.

## Cross-link collections

Connect industries to case studies, glossary terms to service pages, and blog posts to commercial landing pages. Topic clusters compound over time.
