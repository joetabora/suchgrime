If you serve customers across Wisconsin, you need more than a single contact page with your address. **Local SEO at scale** means city hubs, service combinations, and crawl paths that search engines can follow.

## Build a Wisconsin hub

A dedicated [/wisconsin](/wisconsin) page groups your markets by region — Southeast Wisconsin, Madison Metro, Fox Valley — and links to every city page. This concentrates topical authority on your home state.

## Publish city × service matrix pages

Each combination targets long-tail queries: "web development Kenosha WI", "Madison booking system". Matrix pages need **unique intros, FAQs, and internal links** — not duplicate titles.

## Layer schema correctly

Use LocalBusiness on city hubs, Service schema on matrix pages, and FAQ blocks that mirror real buyer questions. Validate with Google's Rich Results Test.
