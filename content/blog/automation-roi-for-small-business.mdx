Automation isn't about replacing people — it's about **removing repetitive work** so small teams compete with larger operators.

## Highest-ROI workflows

1. **Lead intake → CRM** — form submissions tagged and routed automatically  
2. **Booking confirmations** — calendar events plus SMS/email reminders  
3. **Status dashboards** — one view instead of five spreadsheets  

## Start narrow, then expand

Phase one should solve one painful workflow end-to-end. Parlor Desk in our [portfolio](/work/parlor-desk) is a live example of booking admin built for service businesses.

## Connect to your site

Automation gains compound when your marketing site, forms, and internal tools share one data layer — usually PostgreSQL plus event-driven integrations.
